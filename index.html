Stereo Dome Viewer (SBS L|R)

Desktop: drag = pan, wheel = zoom, ←/→ = prev/next, drop images or folders anywhere, S = swap eyes, R = reset.
Quest: hold trigger & move = drag, left stick ↑/↓ = zoom, left stick ←/→ = image FOV (→ wider), right stick ←/→ or A/B (X/Y) = prev/next, grip = reset.
Fisheye uses "Image H FOV" as the lens FOV.
